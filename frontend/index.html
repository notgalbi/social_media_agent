Step 1 — Get your Buffer Token
You only do this once. It takes about 2 minutes.
  1. Go to buffer.com/developers/apps
  2. Click Create an App
  3. Name it anything (e.g. "My Food App")
  4. For Website URL enter: http://localhost
  5. Copy the Access Token shown
Open Buffer Developers →
Step 2 — Connect her Instagram
Inside your Buffer account, connect her Instagram profile.
  1. Go to buffer.com and log in
  2. Click Add a Channel
  3. Select Instagram
  4. Log into her Instagram account
  5. Come back here when done
